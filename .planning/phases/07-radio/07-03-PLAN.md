# 07-03 — Verify and publish Radio

## Objective / context

Publish only verified Radio code/assets; preserve all owner data and other apps.
See 07-01/02 for requirements and design gate.

## Tasks

1. Unit/scoped lint/build/independent review, native browser functional flow and
   separate visual review against the inventory below. Record limitations.
2. Guarded code-only deployment, exact live assets and route HTML/API health,
   CMS/private DB/catalog/analytics invariance. Final GSD record and cleanup.

## QA inventory / success criteria

- Five menu items: native touch cycle/wrap in both directions, retained mobile
  title, Radio glass hero/caption and sound toggle only in menu.
- Radio section: Roots → Y2K → New Era and back with arrows, touch and keyboard;
  title/era/genres visible, first/last arrows disabled correctly, no fake player.
- Entry from menu and return after third era stay centered; direct selected era
  links/reload work. Controls remain focusable, disabled Play is honest.
- Separate screenshots: menu Radio plus every era at 390px, narrow 320px and
  desktop 1440px. No clipped antenna/object/title/caption/controls or overflow.
- Off-happy paths: failed full Radio images recover to inline artwork; failed
  WebGL has honest static Radio fallback. Reduced motion/data remains usable.
- Early loader remains first; Radio does not gate Music; no unsolicited audio
  requests/contexts/stream. Check old Music/video, Code and Dubplates regression.
- Exploratory rapid switching, return/reentry and direction changes at actual
  timing; record screenshot and functional evidence separately.
- Live HTML/status/canonical/sitemap, six asset digests, JS/CSS/health and all
  data hashes unchanged. Physical phone not available; don't claim handset QA.
