# 07-01 — Radio: three eras

## Objective / design gate

Add Radio as the fifth visible main-menu item and a 3D `/radio` section
with three horizontally swipable receivers, all clearly Coming soon.
No real stream, fake playing state, waveform, subscription or audio fetch.

Project stage: finished artist / music / creative-development site.
Audience: listeners exploring MUVS. Objects: menu section and three eras.
Journey: main menu → Radio → choose Roots / Y2K / New Era → future Play.
First decision: era. Success today: discover all three, understand they
are not on air yet, and return to the menu without losing orientation.
Recovery: native prev/next and keyboard alternatives; static fallback
for artwork failure; existing reduced-motion/data and loading behavior.

Keep the existing custom React/Three foundation, not a new UI kit.
Reference: this site's object-led 3D menu and Code cards. Base palette:
slate #696969, mist #a3a3a3, pale #d8d8d8, white #ffffff, ink #111111,
MUVS acid lime #ccff00. Existing Bebas Neue display / Josefin Sans bold
captions / Urbanist controls. No generic card dashboard, extra badges,
fake LIVE indicators or novel animation system. One hero object per view;
quiet bottom controls, legible title/era/genres, honest disabled Play.

Art direction: three genuinely transparent, consistent studio 3D cutouts.
Oldschool: worn 1970s wood/chrome radio, roots/dub stickers and red/gold/green.
Y2K: rounded portable silver/plastic receiver with 1990s–2000s styling.
New Era and hub hero: optical glass shell, visible electronics and lime
accents, modern-retro FM silhouette. No painted floor/shadow or backdrop;
the existing world-floor cast supplies grounding. Three distinct assets,
reuse modern art for the hub. Preserve current user art/videos/presets.

## Tasks — Assets and safe hub plumbing

1. Generate and inspect three built-in-imagegen assets; save final WebP and
   tiny inline fallbacks in the repo; record exact prompts/provenance/hashes.
2. Extend menu cardinality, retained title state and dust rebase safely;
   preserve old visible order, initial-load priorities, wrapping, camera
   lanes, existing videos and opt-in menu audio.

## Success criteria

Three distinct genuinely transparent WebP objects and matching inline fallbacks
exist with recorded provenance. Existing menu order gains Radio at the end,
wraps without a dust jump, retains fifth-title state and preserves initial
Music/video readiness. See 07-02-PLAN for the Radio surface and 07-03 for QA.

## Known constraints

Existing 56-unit section depth is not the carousel period; keep it.
Physical hub item order is reversed from visual swipe order. New artwork
must not become an additional initial Music loading gate. Existing image
and camera effects stay under their current owner-selected settings.
