# 06-01 — Menu sound-design draft

## Objective

Publish a restrained opt-in sound-design draft on muvs.dev: a soft pad/vinyl bed and short section-character transitions in the3D menu, controlled by a bottom-right button. Hide the control and silence the layer outside the menu, especially while the music player is available. Keep the current visuals, content, startup speed and unrelated server applications intact.

## Context / UX gate

- Finished artist/creative-development site, mobile-first exploratory3D menu. Existing design, fonts and gray palette are the reference; no UI-library migration.
- First choice: sound remains off until a deliberate button activation. Native button,44px target, visible focus and truthful loading/retry states. No permission popup, autoplay bypass, forced sound or preloader delay.
- Menu only means the main Music/Mixes/Code/About hub. Sections, legacy pages, admin and Dubplates remain unchanged/silent. Selection changes may have restrained spatial cues, not continuous gesture-triggered spam.
- Real licensedCC0 assets preferred; track credits/source/license and transformations. Never modify artist uploads/masters or copy samples with ambiguous redistribution rights.
- No physical iPhone access is implied by emulated browser tests. Safari-compatible explicit WebAudio unlock and interrupted-state recovery must fail silently/retryably.

## Tasks

1. **Assets and engine:** choose/prepare compact CC0 pad and four short transitions; document provenance/recipe/seam/level checks. Build a lazy opt-in one-context audio engine with capped gains, fades, no late queued effects and race-safe lifecycle tests.
2. **Menu integration:** add a matching bottom-right sound toggle, accepted-navigation start/end wiring and menu/visibility/loading/player gates. Preserve scene/film timing and keyboard/touch ownership. Test normal/failure/rapid-toggle/return/hidden flows and mobile/desktop placement.
3. **Publish and handoff:** build/full/scoped checks, independent review, native browser media/network/state evidence, guarded deployment and exact live assets/content invariance. Record the draft's subjective-audition limitation and how to enable it.

## Success criteria

- No audio context, fetch or sound before opt-in; no new sound assets in critical loader preloads.
- Toggle immediately mutes/cancels pending work, reports blocked/failed loading honestly and allows retry.
- Ambient is seamless/quiet; a single bounded transition follows accepted navigation, with no backlog or stale-ID interruption.
- Button/audio absent in sections/player/admin/Dubplates; return remembers the current document's choice, hidden tabs pause, and unmount disposes resources.
- Video autoplay remains muted and unchanged. No production test track plays/downloads or CMS/preset writes.
- Tests/build/visual/functional/live checks pass; source changes and metadata committed atomically. Physical device/audio-aesthetic claims only when actually verified.
