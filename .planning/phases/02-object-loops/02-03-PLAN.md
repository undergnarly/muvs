# 02-03 — One-shot artwork entrances

## Objective
On each deliberate selection of Music, Mixes or Code, play a restrained 2–3 second entrance, then continue the existing ambient video loop. Music settles from a small height with dust; Mixes settles from a brighter warm glow; only Code's laptop lid opens, and its regular white-blue face light becomes more apparent.

## Tasks
1. Prepare stronger Code lighting and exact original lid/foreground layers with a localized Flow cleanplate behind the lid. Preserve every other part of the sculpture.
2. Implement selected-only, once-per-selection entrance lifecycle and R3F effects in the current render loop. Keep navigation interruptible, one playing decoder, stable scene/camera, reduced-motion/data-saving fallbacks and no Pause control.
3. Verify media/lifecycle unit tests, build, desktop/mobile touch, interruption/return/hidden/failure behavior; publish only after visual acceptance. Record evidence and atomic commits.

## UX gate
Finished creative portfolio, existing 3D selection surface. Visitors choose Music/Mixes/Code by swipe or existing navigation. Preserve labels, composition, camera and click targets. These requested 2–3s decorative entrances are intentionally longer than control-feedback timings; they must never delay navigation. Recovery remains a static original poster. Existing Three/R3F is the foundation; no animation-library migration or new page/UI controls.
